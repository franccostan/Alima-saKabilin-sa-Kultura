About Us

Alima sa Kabilin sa Kultura

Alima sa Kabilin sa Kultura is a community extension project of Cebu Technological University - Danao Campus, dedicated to preserving the local cultural heritage of Barangay Catarman. Through cultural mapping across its puroks, the project documents heirloom artifacts, photographs, and personal narratives passed down through generations.

Every artifact tells a story — of hardship, craftsmanship, and identity. We believe the young generation deserves to know their history and carry it forward with pride.
